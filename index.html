A little about me.

Hello, I'm flku, formerly known as ohdee or 2lonely. I'm a professional reverse engineer and co-founder of Qernix, the best reverse engineering team. I do reverse engineering, loader/bot security, SDKs, UI, and basically everything you want.

I've worked with some of the biggest Rocket League bot projects and currently have ~50 high-end bot models, including complete project dumps. I started with Legacy, a popular Rocket League lag-switch, before moving into Rift's loader security. After falling out with Kubi, I released the models I had cracked on GitHub as revenge.

These days, I'm working on loaders for Sirius, mxphl's 4.2T-step bot. Sirius V1 already beat Mawkzy, while V3 completely destroys V1 in a 1v1. Mech, mxphl's previous project, also beat Rift 10–0, so you can probably imagine what Sirius does to Rift now.

And yeah, there's a little bit of skidding involved. I can provide bot source code and models for anyone wanting to implement their own rewards, features, or ideas. :)
